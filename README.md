# AutoForge-Sentinel
AI-based industrial quality prediction digital twin with live conveyor simulation, sensor alerts, downtime prediction and backend data logging.
https://autoforge-sentinel-mayank-20260930.mayankrajput244251.chatgpt.site/


AutoForge-Sentinel is an AI-based industrial quality prediction and monitoring system designed to simulate smart manufacturing environments through a digital twin of an industrial conveyor system. The project integrates real-time conveyor simulation, sensor-based monitoring, automated defect alerts, downtime prediction, and backend data logging to demonstrate how intelligent automation can improve industrial production quality, operational efficiency, and equipment reliability.

The system focuses on identifying potential quality issues and abnormal operating conditions during the manufacturing process. By combining simulated sensor data, predictive analytics, and visual monitoring, AutoForge-Sentinel aims to provide an interactive representation of how Industry 4.0 technologies can support smarter industrial decision-making.

Key features include an interactive conveyor simulation, sensor status monitoring, quality prediction, defect detection alerts, downtime analysis, and structured backend data management. The project demonstrates the practical application of digital twins, industrial IoT concepts, predictive maintenance, and AI-driven quality control in modern manufacturing systems.

AutoForge-Sentinel is developed as a practical engineering project to explore the integration of mechanical and industrial processes with intelligent monitoring technologies. It highlights how digital simulation and predictive systems can help reduce production defects, identify potential equipment failures, improve maintenance planning, and support more reliable manufacturing operations.

**Project Goal:** To demonstrate a smart industrial quality monitoring and prediction system that combines digital simulation, sensor intelligence, and data-driven decision support for next-generation manufacturing.

## ⚙️ How AutoForge-Sentinel Works

**AutoForge-Sentinel — AI-Powered Industrial Quality Prediction & Monitoring**

AutoForge-Sentinel demonstrates an intelligent industrial workflow that combines conveyor simulation, sensor monitoring, AI-based risk prediction, and automated response mechanisms to support smarter manufacturing.

### 🔄 Five-Step Working Process

**1. Drive Motor**  
The motor powers the conveyor system and initiates the industrial production process.

**2. Move Part**  
The conveyor transports components through the production line for inspection and monitoring.

**3. Read Sensors**  
Sensors collect operational data to monitor component movement and identify abnormal conditions.

**4. Predict Risk**  
The AI-based prediction module analyzes available data to identify potential quality issues, operational risks, and possible downtime.

**5. Take Action**  
The system presents alerts and supports timely corrective action to improve production reliability and reduce potential defects.

### 🚀 Key Highlights

- **Smart Conveyor Simulation:** Visualizes the movement of components through an industrial workflow.
- **Sensor-Based Monitoring:** Tracks simulated operating conditions and sensor status.
- **Predictive Risk Analysis:** Demonstrates how data-driven intelligence can help identify potential production problems.
- **Automated Alerts:** Highlights abnormal conditions that may require attention.
- **Industrial Digital Twin Concept:** Represents a digital version of an industrial process for monitoring and analysis.
- **Backend Data Logging:** Supports the recording and management of relevant system data.

### 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of AutoForge-Sentinel is to demonstrate how **Artificial Intelligence, Industrial IoT, Digital Twin technology, and predictive monitoring** can work together to improve industrial quality control and manufacturing efficiency.

*This project is a practical exploration of intelligent manufacturing concepts, focusing on early risk identification, improved operational visibility, and smarter industrial decision-making.*
